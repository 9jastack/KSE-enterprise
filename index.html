Does KSE provide loans directly?

KSE facilitates access to SME funding through partner financial institutions. Eligibility, rates and approval decisions are determined by the relevant institution.

What should I bring to an initial conversation?

A short description of what you need is a good start. We’ll let you know if any further information would be useful for your enquiry.

Can I ask about Proof of Funds or Point of Entry support?

Yes. Get in touch with the purpose and requirements you’ve been given. We’ll discuss the documentation support available and explain the next step.
